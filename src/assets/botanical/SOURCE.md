# Asset provenance

Generated with OpenAI image generation for the owner's botanical-library request.
These are photographic-style generated cutouts, not photographs of the paintings
or taxonomically verified botanical specimens.

Three earlier assets (green serrated leaf, curled leaf, pink rose petal) are reused
byte-for-byte from `public/botanical/`. Those legacy originals remain unchanged.
Nine additional assets were generated on November 4, 2026: dark green, gold,
burnt-orange and deep-red leaves; deep-red rose, pale-pink/peach carnation,
spotted orange tiger lily and cream petals. Original generated sheets remain in
the execution workspace; only optimized individual cutouts are served.

Processing: separate cells without clipping the subject, clear near-zero alpha
background residue, trim transparent space, proportionally resize to a maximum
192px plus a 4px transparent border, and export quality-84 alpha-WebP. No artwork
photographs or botanical colors were changed. Edges are reviewed against ivory
and dark green. Individual natural dimensions and byte counts are in the registry.
