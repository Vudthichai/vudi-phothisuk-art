# Regal Collection first pass

Five paintings imported from the artist-provided VPA Website Update archive.
The archive originals remain unchanged. Full canvas and white border are retained;
cloth outside the canvas is removed with measured four-corner rectification on
Carnations, Navy, Sahara, and Verdant. Ciaran uses the existing clean full view.
See regal-photo-curation.json for source mappings and corner coordinates.

The collection uses a charcoal-green opening, ivory exhibition rows, muted gold
accents, a five-work jump index, and a closing inquiry link. Individual routes
include the full artwork, edited narrative based on the artist's source story,
dimensions, medium, neighboring works, and one original close-up each.
No artwork or room imagery was generated. The unmatched IMG_4278.JPG is excluded
pending identification; duplicate views are retained in the source archive.

Source text and sales status came from the existing migration inventory. Ciaran's
24 by 36 inch dimensions, medium, and honor/integrity/leadership story were newly
confirmed at https://www.vudiphothisukart.com/ciaran on October 29, 2026.
Carnations and Navy retain their published Sold labels; other works use inquiry
links without asserting availability or prices. Narratives are proposed editorial
copy for artist review. No production merge is implied.

Validation: TypeScript, 23 tests, production build, asset existence and dimensions,
desktop collection/detail visual review, and 390px collection layout review.
