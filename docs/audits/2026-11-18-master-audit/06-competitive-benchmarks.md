# Competitive benchmark research — Vudi Phothisuk Art

Research date: November 18, 2026 (America/New_York). Read-only public-source research. This document is research input, not a change proposal or valuation.

## Method and limits

The cohort comprises **25 artists**: six Pittsburgh/Western Pennsylvania comparators, six PA/OH/NY regional comparators, six US national practice/business-model comparators, and seven international comparators. Geography is not a career ranking: New York-based nationally recognized artists appear in either regional or national groups according to the comparison being made. Advanced artists are **aspirational practice/presentation benchmarks**, not equivalent sales or valuation peers for an emerging artist.

Facts below are **Verified (public-source)** only to the extent specified. An artist's own biography/CV is first-party evidence of what that artist claims; a museum, university, presenting gallery, or publisher page is separate corroboration of that specific relationship. A historical exhibition is not proof of current representation. A gallery artist listing is not proof of exclusivity. Prices are public asking/listing prices, not verified sales. No traffic, revenue, follower totals, search positions, or current auction valuations have been inferred.

Website comments are **Inferred** from retrieved navigation, project/artwork information, and acquisition pathways. This pass did not run a full browser/performance/accessibility audit of 25 external websites. Their Core Web Vitals, mobile behavior, checkout completion, accessibility conformance, and search rankings are **Not Yet Verified**. Social-account presence and press listings are not treated as audience engagement or independent critical endorsement. Missing evidence below means not verified in this pass, not that the artist lacks it.

## Transferable conclusions for Vudi (Inferred)

1. A polished website is one part of a professional record. The strongest local benchmarks connect named projects, material specificity, dates/venues and independent host documentation; these are more defensible than broad premium/luxury claims.
2. Gallery/inquiry selling can be credible without full checkout. Armbruster and ZYNKA make the next contact step explicit; incomplete Square/POD integration should not itself be labeled a website bug.
3. Separate original painting, artist-made editioned print, reproduction and commissioned installation. Copeland’s signed hand-screenprinted edition is not comparable to a POD reproduction solely because both cost around tens of dollars.
4. Donoughe’s transparent asking prices show that collectors can receive clear acquisition information, but his career, media, sizes and exhibition history prevent using those asks as a direct valuation formula for Vudi.
5. Add substantiated exhibition/process/collaboration documentation before using institutional recognition or luxury-placement language. Do not manufacture credentials to imitate the benchmarks.

No competitor Core Web Vitals, follower counts, actual sales, search ranks, or buyer conversion rates were measured. Full current retail catalogs and legal representation agreements were not available; only observed public information is asserted.

## What the comparison supports—and what it does not

1. **Distinctiveness requires a narrower claim.** Relief, sculptural botanical imagery and tactile mixed media are established practices, as the cited work of Zhuang, Hwang, Carrington, Hashimoto and Clary demonstrates. This does not imply copying or diminish Vudi's authorship. It means the strongest positioning will specify Vudi's particular combination of sculpted flora, landscape, embedded narratives, changing light and personal decisions, with exact material/process evidence.
2. **A polished website is an entry condition, not independent career validation.** The advanced cohort's stronger signal is documented bodies of work, chronological exhibitions, institutional records, critical writing and relationships that can be independently checked. Photography, mockups and typography cannot substitute for those records.
3. **Small reproducible products and original objects are different markets.** Morin's hand-signed 8 × 10 print at $40, Oswald's much smaller framed watercolor at $225, Clary's framed paper relief at $2,700, and Begum's signed edition-of-40 screenprint at £1,200 are materially/editionally different objects. None establishes the correct asking price for a Vudi original or unsigned open-edition reproduction. Currency conversion, tax, shipping, framing, provenance, medium, demand and realized sales have not been normalized.
4. **The most useful near-term examples are operational.** Oswald's exact art/frame measurements and commission/licensing separation; Paradigm's object details and collector list; Morin's print paper/signature description; Jean's collector FAQ; and Law's written acquisition terms can inform a future approved implementation without imitating another artist's identity.
5. **Social and SEO conclusions remain bounded.** These artist identities surfaced under targeted name queries in the research tool; that is observed retrieval in one tool, not a Google ranking test or an AI-citation benchmark. No comparative audience, traffic, conversion or market-share claims are warranted.

## Coverage gaps requiring a separate pass

- Complete visual/mobile/performance/accessibility inspection of all external artist sites.
- Independent verification of every self-published CV claim and current gallery contracts (not possible from public pages alone).
- Social profile consistency and current activity across all 25 artists; no follower counts should be invented.
- Current prices and edition terms where not exposed in source text; no asking-price extrapolation from auction aggregates.
- Controlled, repeatable search and AI-answer visibility measurements, with geography, login state, date and query held constant.
- Collector/sales evidence for Vudi: actual invoices, commissions, repeat collectors, trade inquiries, exhibition attendance and independent reviews. This is the missing basis for market-traction comparisons, not permission to assume no sales.
