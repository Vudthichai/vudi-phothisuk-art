### Where Vudi realistically stands

On available evidence, **an emerging artist with a coherent, locally active direct-inquiry practice and stronger-than-basic digital presentation**. The event sources show public participation and a history of community/fundraising engagement. They do not establish a sustained represented-artist market. This is a usable starting position, not a deficiency to cover with more elaborate design.

For meaningful **regional** advancement: finish catalog records, produce excellent in-person show documentation, build relationships with relevant curators/galleries/designers, pursue appropriate juried/group/residency opportunities and maintain a dated CV. For **national** recognition: show a sustained body of evolving work, third-party critical/curatorial writing, repeat professional opportunities and collector/provenance records. For **international** recognition: independently meaningful exhibitions, representation or institutional/curatorial relationships and feasible cross-border acquisition/transport—not simply an international shipping toggle or expensive price point. These are milestones, not guaranteed outcomes or a requirement to imitate famous comparators.

## Conversion judgment

The experience communicates care and authenticity. The strongest conversion improvement is **less uncertainty at the moment of inquiry**, not stronger selling language. Keep the art prominent and the design restrained.

The principal observed friction is the large first-visit consent overlay, long collection/Available Art journeys, the non-link inquiry phrase on Forest cards, and missing object/acquisition facts. Long pages and Coming Soon are not inherently defects; their effect on inquiries has not been measured. No abandonment, conversion-rate or revenue-lift estimate is warranted.

Recommended future collector path: clear object/status → full/detail image → concise acquisition explanation → contextual inquiry → timely human follow-up. A copy-email fallback or small optional inquiry form may help webmail users later, but do not add collection of personal data or a complex form by default. No messages were sent during this audit.
