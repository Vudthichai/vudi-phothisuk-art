## Remaining verification register

- **Domains:** probe hyphenated host and default Netlify alias from another network, then confirm ownership/DNS from read-only owner records. The environment's 403 tunnel response is not an origin diagnosis.
- **Search:** GSC/Bing ownership, sitemap receipt, URL Inspection and actual indexed coverage; controlled local/unbranded search and AI-answer observations. Existing snippets may be stale.
- **Performance:** representative mobile throttling, real-device Safari/Retina, field p75 LCP/INP/CLS and consent-respecting inquiry attribution. No numerical field score is claimed.
- **Accessibility:** screen-reader journeys, comprehensive contrast and touch/focus audit on real devices. Automated structure and keyboard mechanisms are only part of conformance.
- **Art objects:** confirm current stock, Dragon Lily facts, completed object dates, medium/depth/weight/back/side/hanging/care/condition, and approved acquisition documentation.
- **Professional record:** artist-provided CV/programs/press and independent venue/gallery/institution corroboration. No source of realized sales or repeat collectors supplied.
- **Print commerce:** actual sample quality, final specification, vendor costs, fulfillment/shipping/returns/payment/tax decisions and precise profit/donation accounting; veterans recipient remains TBA.
- **Competitors:** full rendered/mobile site audits, current exclusive representation, complete social consistency and realized sales. Public listing prices must not become valuation claims.
