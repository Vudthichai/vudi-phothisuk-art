# Curated Oceans artwork pages

All eleven Oceans artwork pages follow this sequence:
1. Existing full painting photograph, story, dimensions, and availability.
2. One distinct room study, separated by a subtle rule and balanced spacing.
3. Optional existing craftsmanship image, shown in full without forced zoom.
4. Existing previous/next navigation, with its closing rule.

The RoomStudy component pairs every painting with its own interior and short
visual pairing copy. Ten new empty-room backgrounds complement the existing
Beach Day boho room. The actual hero photograph is overlaid as a separate image;
no painting is regenerated, recolored, cropped, or distorted. Room scale is
explicitly illustrative. Original assets and earlier staged images remain in the
repository but are not displayed as duplicate room studies.

Main artwork records, narratives, prices/status, dimensions, and collection
presentation are unchanged. No interior consultation service is added. The new
copy describes the visual relationship between artwork and room only.

Validation covers all eleven room mappings and source images, unique settings,
section order, optional details, and existing navigation. Desktop portrait and
landscape room compositions and a 390px browser layout were inspected. Physical
phone review and artist review of each setting remain valuable.
